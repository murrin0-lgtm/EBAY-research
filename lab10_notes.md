Ethan attacks the 4% annual revenue growth. He asked why assume it will grow 4% every year when eBay's historical growth ranged from 1.69% to 7.95%? What evidence would make you change that number?
I chose 4% because it is a moderate assumption. It is within the historical range and I'd rather not just assume that history will repeat itself. I would revise the number if different trends occused or changes showed growth being higher or lower consistently.
I chose to attack Oracle's annual capital spending decreasing from 92.5 billion in FY2027 to 40 billion in FY2031 while revenue keeps growing. I asked why that number, and what would change it. He said he assumed Oracle's large investments in the first few years would create capacity that could support future revenue, allowing annual spending to decrease as those facilities become operational.
